# Third-party components

- **PyMuPDF / MuPDF** provide page composition, complex text shaping, and rendering. PyMuPDF is offered under AGPL and commercial licensing terms. Consult its distribution and upstream license for applicable terms.
- **Pillow** provides image reading and contact-sheet generation under its own license.
- **Fonts and source comics** are user-supplied inputs with separate rights. They are not distributed here, and this project's license does not cover them.

No source manga artwork, translated commercial chapter, font binary, original user's private configuration, or authentication information is part of this public package.
